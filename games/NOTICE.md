# The included games

The files in this folder are the original DOS releases of *Wing Commander* (`wc1`, with *The
Secret Missions* 1 and 2) and *Wing Commander II: Vengeance of the Kilrathi* (`wc2`, with
*Special Operations* 1 and 2).

They are copyrighted works of Electronic Arts Inc., first published by Origin Systems, and are
included in Confederation by permission of Electronic Arts. They are not covered by
Confederation's license, the GNU General Public License, and may not be sold or charged for. See
[NOTICE.md](../NOTICE.md).

As the permission asks, each program is renamed:

| Here | Originally |
|---|---|
| `wc1/CONFWC1.EXE` | `WC.EXE`, *Wing Commander* and *The Secret Missions* |
| `wc1/CONFSM2.EXE` | `SM2.EXE`, *The Secret Missions 2* |
| `wc1/CONFTRN1.EXE` | `TRANSFER.EXE`, a transfer tool for saved games |
| `wc1/CONFTRN2.EXE` | `TRANS2.EXE`, a transfer tool for saved games |
| `wc2/CONFWC2.EXE` | `WC2.EXE`, *Wing Commander II* |
| `wc2/CONFSO1.EXE` | `SO1.EXE`, *Special Operations 1* |
| `wc2/CONFSO2.EXE` | `SO2.EXE`, *Special Operations 2* |

The rest is as the games came: their data in `GAMEDAT`, and the settings their installers wrote
(`WINGCMDR.CFG`, `CRUSADE.CFG`, `wc2.cfg`).
