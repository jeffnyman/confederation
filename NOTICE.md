## Confederation

Confederation is © 2026 Jeff Nyman. It is free software, licensed under the GNU General Public
License, version 3 ([LICENSE](LICENSE)).

Confederation is a DOS PC emulator for the *Wing Commander* games, made to preserve the original
games as they were. It currently plays *Wing Commander* with *The Secret Missions* 1 and 2, and
*Wing Commander II: Vengeance of the Kilrathi* with *Special Operations* 1 and 2, which are
included in this repository. It can also play *Wing Commander III: Heart of the Tiger*, which is
not included: it plays a copy you have.
